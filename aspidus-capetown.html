African Gateway in Cape Town

Aspidus Commodities Traders (Pty) Ltd, based in Cape Town, South Africa, serves as our strategic gateway to the rich natural resources of the African continent, specializing in precious metals, minerals, and agricultural commodities.

Why Cape Town?

Cape Town offers unique advantages that position Aspidus as a leader in African commodity trade, linking local resources to global markets.

Gateway to African Resources

Cape Town is strategically located to access Southern and Sub-Saharan Africa's vast natural resource. This allows Aspidus to efficiently source and export a diverse range of commodities, from minerals to agricultural products, to global markets.

  • Rich Resource Access: Unlocking Africa's mineral and agricultural wealth.
  • Strategic Geographical Position: Ideal for sourcing and export.
  • Global Market Connection: Bridging Africa with international demand.

Established Trade Infrastructure

South Africa boasts robust financial and logistical infrastructure, including well-developed ports and a regulated financial sector. Aspidus Commodities Traders leverages this stability to ensure secure and efficient commodity trading operations.

  • Port Facilities: Efficient cargo handling and shipping.
  • Financial Sector: Secure and transparent transactions.
  • Stable Regulatory Environment: Promoting fair and legal trade.
